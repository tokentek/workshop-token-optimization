# SNAKE-02: Eat and grow

Size: S

## Story

As a player, I want the snake to eat food and grow, so that the game becomes harder as I play.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Given a new game, when the game starts, then one food item shows on a random cell that the snake does not cover.
2. Given food on the grid, when the head of the snake moves onto the food, then the snake grows by one segment.
3. Given the snake ate the food, when the next tick comes, then a new food item shows on a random free cell.
4. Given the snake covers most of the grid, when new food is placed, then the food never shows on a cell of the snake.
